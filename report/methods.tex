\section{Materials and methods}

\subsection{Dataset}
We analysed publicly released \emph{movement execution} EEG from~\cite{ofner2017upper} (BNCI Horizon~2020 accession~\texttt{001-2017}; see also \texttt{docs/DatabaseDescription.pdf}).
Healthy adults performed cue-guided sustained actions of elbow, forearm, and hand synergy families together with fixation \emph{rest}; recordings sampled 61 EEG channels at 512\,Hz with auxiliary kinematics~\cite{ofner2017upper}.

Each run corresponds to an EEGLAB \texttt{ME\_Sxx\_rYY.mat} archive read by~\texttt{EEGMatLoader}.
We retain 21 motor ribbon electrodes defined in~\texttt{src/config.py}, resample to 128\,Hz, and extract epochs from $-0.5$\,s to $+2.0$\,s relative to cue onset.
Trials exceeding $\pm$200\,$\mu$V amplitude limits are discarded.

\textbf{Aggregation to four multiclass targets.}
Opposite movement directions collapse to symmetric families: elbow flexion/extension~\(\rightarrow\) \emph{elbow}; forearm supination/pronation~\(\rightarrow\) \emph{forearm}; hand open/close~\(\rightarrow\) \emph{hand}; \emph{rest} remains singleton (event codes enumerated in~\texttt{EVENT\_ID}).
Chance performance is therefore 25\%.

\subsection{Preprocessing and derived tensors}
The export script~\texttt{scripts/build\_dataset.py} applies~\texttt{EEGPreprocessor} with notch filtering at line frequency, followed by zero-phase FIR band-pass filtering between 0.5 and 40\,Hz (MNE; \texttt{phase='zero'}).
\textbf{Note:} exported tensors omit common-average re-referencing (\texttt{apply\_car=False}) to match the convolutional branch; CSP hyperparameter sweeps in~\texttt{scripts/tune\_csp\_preprocessing.py} may employ alternative band definitions and should be interpreted as exploratory rather than strictly identical preprocessing.

For each surviving trial:
\begin{itemize}
\setlength\itemsep{0pt}
  \item raw tensors undergo per-channel, per-trial temporal $z$-scoring prior to PSD and coherence extraction;
  \item \textbf{PSD tensors} (\texttt{X\_psd}): Welch estimates over 3--30\,Hz;
  \item \textbf{connectivity tensors} (\texttt{X\_conn}): log-transformed magnitude of complex Morlet cross-spectral density at 10 and 22\,Hz for upper-triangular channel pairs (\texttt{src/processing.py}), followed by dataset-wide standardisation.
\end{itemize}

Figure~\ref{fig:pipe} summarises integration of optional branches into convolutional fusion networks.
Stratified splitting (train $\approx$\,85\%, test $\approx$\,15\%, random seed 42) preserves class proportions.

\textbf{Convolutional baselines.}
\textbf{EEGNet} adopts separable convolutional motifs from~\cite{lawhern2018eegnet} as implemented in~\texttt{src/networks.py}.

\textbf{\texttt{EEGPsdNet}} couples the EEGNet waveform encoder with a lightweight spectral CNN on~\texttt{X\_psd}; representations are fused by vector concatenation and batch normalization before the terminal linear layer.

\textbf{\texttt{EEGConnectivityNet}} compresses waveform and connectivity embeddings, applies a softmax attention gate to mix modalities, and passes the fused vector through a multilayer perceptron classifier.

\textbf{Optimisation.} All deep models minimise class-weighted cross-entropy with label smoothing, optimise with AdamW (stepped learning-rate reduction on plateau), inject Gaussian augmentation noise on EEG tensors during training, and clip gradient norms; see~\texttt{scripts/train\_eeg*.py}.

\subsection{t-SNE of penultimate embeddings}
Activation vectors feeding the terminal affine layer~(pre-softmax) were extracted for withheld trials across architectures.
We projected them using scikit-learn's \texttt{TSNE} with matched hyper-parameters to obtain comparable two-dimensional neighbourhoods.

\subsection{Connectivity scalp visualisation}

For qualitative neurophysiological corroboration, trial-averaged connectivity-derived quantities were interpolated to scalp coordinates to visualise contrasts between \emph{hand} movement epochs and \emph{rest}, using the Graz channel montage~\cite{ofner2017upper}.
