\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\textbf{Block~I versus Block~II.}
Table~\ref{tab:main} reports narrow-band exploratory CSP near \(\sim 38\mbox{--}39\%\) withheld accuracy, broadband CSP on tensors sharing the \(\sim\)0.5\,--\,40\,Hz envelope with convolutional preprocessing at \(\sim 32\%\), and Block~II models on identical splits~\cite{gramfort2013meg,lawhern2018eegnet}.
Convolutional receptive fields improve by \(\sim\)17\,--\,18 percentage points versus the optimised shallow CSP line and \(\sim\)24 versus broadband CSP paired to identical envelopes.
This pattern suggests discriminative cues that exceed short CSP log-variance summaries of surface waveforms~\cite{gramfort2013meg}.

\textbf{CSP preprocessing parity.}
The CSP rows entail different contracts: exploratory fits concentrate variance inside motor-frequency hypotheses, whereas broadband CSP aligns band limiting with Block~II.
Separately, tensor preparation here omitted common-average referencing for convolutional pipelines~\cite{gramfort2013meg}.
Fair CSP--CNN benchmarking must therefore unify band-pass envelopes, referencing, and cropping before blaming spatial filtering deficits alone.

\textbf{Multimodal fusion.}
Welch concatenation and coherence attention each add roughly five percentage points over the EEGNet-style baseline~\cite{lawhern2018eegnet}.
Auxiliary maps likely supply marginal complementary constraints rather than orthogonal geometry.

\textbf{Neurophysiological structure versus accuracy.}
Figure~\ref{fig:connTopo} exhibits organised peri-central contrast between trial-averaged hand-related epochs and rest~\cite{ofner2017upper} while Table~\ref{tab:main} shows only modest incremental gains from fusion.
Interpretable connectivity structure and softmax margins are therefore not interchangeable metrics.

\textbf{Embeddings and residual errors.}
Colour intermixing in Fig.~\ref{fig:tsne} parallels the accuracy plateau: softmax precursors from different classes remain neighbour points for every Block~II model.
Confusion matrices (Fig.~\ref{fig:cms}) sharpen diagonals with depth, with remaining errors concentrated among kinematically related synergies.

\textbf{Generalisation scope.}
Withholding trials from a pooled subject mix is optimistic relative to leave-one-subject-out evaluation recommended for neurotechnology translation~\cite{ofner2017upper}.
