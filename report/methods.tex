\section{Materials and methods}
\label{sec:methods}

\subsection{Dataset and common preprocessing}
\label{sec:dataprep}

We analysed openly released movement--execution EEG~\cite{ofner2017upper} recorded while volunteers sustained upper-limb actions with elbow, forearm, hand synergies, and resting fixation~(BNCI Horizon~2020, accession \texttt{001-2017}; 61 electrodes at \(512\)~Hz~\cite{ofner2017upper}).

Experiments retained twenty-one peri-central electrodes, resampled the continuous signal at \(128\)~Hz, and extracted cues locked to \([-0.5,\,2.0]\)\,s relative to the stimulus.
Trials with peak EEG magnitude outside \(\pm 200~\mu\text{V}\) prior to augmentation were discarded.
Mirror movement directions~(flexion/extension, supination/pronation, open/close) were pooled, yielding four classes: \emph{elbow}, \emph{hand}, \emph{forearm}, and \emph{rest}.

All trainable architectures shared one stratified random partition of pooled subjects: \(\sim\)85\% of trials optimisation, \(\sim\)15\% validation, fixed pseudo-random seed for comparable convergence noise.

\textbf{Auxiliary spectral and connectivity tensors.}
After per-trial, per-electrode temporal normalization, Welch spectra between 3 and 30~Hz constitute log-magnitude PSD maps.
Connectivity descriptors arise from modulus of pairwise complex Morlet cross-spectral densities at 10 and 22~Hz summed over unordered electrode pairs~(then globally standardised).
These tensors feed only the multimodal convolutional hybrids.

\textbf{Broadband filtering.}
Recording segments were mains-notch filtered followed by FIR band-pass filtering between \(0.5\) and \(40\)~Hz~(zero-phase MNE kernels).
CNN exports omitted common-average re-referencing whereas supplementary CSP optimisation sweeps exercised alternative motor-band hypotheses; divergence between those variants is flagged in Discussion.

\subsection{Block~I --- CSP with LDA}
\label{sec:block1}

Block~I implements the canonical motor-BCI blueprint: CSP spatial filters optimised for logarithmic-band-power variance ratios and immediately followed by linear discriminant analysis~\cite{gramfort2013meg}.
Six orthogonal spatial patterns provided feature vectors \(\mathbb{R}^6\) for LDA.

\subsection{Block~II --- convolutional EEG networks}
\label{sec:block2}

Block~II instantiates differentiable temporal receptive fields analogous to EEGNet~\cite{lawhern2018eegnet}:
\begin{enumerate}
\setlength\itemsep{0pt}
  \item \textbf{Baseline CNN} --- separable convolutional stem processing filtered EEG epoch tensors~\cite{lawhern2018eegnet}.
  \item \textbf{Spectral concat fusion}~(EEGPSDNet style) --- late fusion by vector concatenation of CNN embeddings with shallow spectral CNN embeddings on Welch maps, batch-normalised prior to softmax.
  \item \textbf{Connectivity attention fusion}~(EEGConnectivityNet style) --- independent encoders squeeze waveform embeddings and coherence tensors, softmax attention mixtures modulate modality weights, lastly MLP softmax classification.
\end{enumerate}

All deep architectures were trained by minimising weighted cross-entropy with mild label smoothing, using adaptive moment~(AdamW-type) updates with weight decay, reducing learning rates on withheld-loss plateaus, applying additive Gaussian EEG noise augmentation, and clipping gradients.
Checkpoints retaining maximum withheld-set accuracy were kept for inference.

\subsection{Ancillary visualisation protocols}

Withheld softmax precursors underwent two-dimensional t-SNE with matched hyper-parameters across Block~II models.
Connectivity scalp maps summarise trial-level coupling-derived quantities averaged separately for pooled hand epochs and pooled rest epochs prior to planar interpolation~\cite{ofner2017upper}.
