\section{Conclusion}

We contrasted Block~I CSP+LDA~\cite{gramfort2013meg} with Block~II convolutional decoders~\cite{lawhern2018eegnet} using identical pooled-stratified withheld partitions~\cite{ofner2017upper}.

Table~\ref{tab:main} summarises withheld accuracies; interpretation of embeddings, topographies, fusion limits, and generalisation occupies Section~\ref{sec:discussion}.

Recommended follow-ups are aligned preprocessing for classical and deep pipelines, strictly subject-independent benchmarks~\cite{ofner2017upper}, and fusion designs that exploit auxiliary tensors without weakening temporal discriminability.
