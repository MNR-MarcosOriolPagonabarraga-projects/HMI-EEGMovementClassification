================================================================================

================================================================================
6. DISCUSSION (~0.75 PAGE)
================================================================================

THIS IS THE MOST IMPORTANT SECTION.

================================================================================
6.1 The Dual-Branch Dilemma
================================================================================

Core hypothesis:

"The optimizer converged toward the high-dimensional temporal branch because it provided stronger gradients and easier memorization pathways."

Explain:
- Raw EEG is noisy but information-rich.
- Deep networks overfit to noise.
- Clean engineered features become underutilized.

This is the central scientific contribution.

================================================================================
6.2 EEG Constraints
================================================================================

Discuss:
- Volume conduction
- Low spatial resolution
- Single-trial noise
- Inter-subject variability

Explain:
- 4-class motor decoding from scalp EEG is intrinsically difficult.

================================================================================
6.3 Why Connectivity Still Matters
================================================================================

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT claim connectivity failed completely.

Instead say:
- Connectivity features revealed stable motor-network organization.
- Neurophysiological consistency validates the extracted representations.
- Classification metrics alone do not capture feature quality.

================================================================================
6.4 Future Work
================================================================================

Recommended future direction:
- Remove raw temporal branch entirely.
- Train lightweight topology-only MLP.
- Strong regularization.
- Increase dataset size.
- Subject-specific adaptation.
- Temporal smoothing across trials.
