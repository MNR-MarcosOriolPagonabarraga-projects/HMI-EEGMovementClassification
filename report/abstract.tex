\begin{abstract}
We compare classical Common Spatial Patterns with linear discrimination~(Block~I) against convolutional EEG decoders~(Block~II), including an EEGNet-style baseline and dual-branch models that fuse raw epochs with Welch spectra or wavelet coherence~\cite{ofner2017upper,lawhern2018eegnet}.
All setups share pooled-stratified train--validation partitions on cue-locked upper-limb EEG.
Quantitative scores and visuals are in Sections~\ref{sec:results}--\ref{sec:discussion}.
Code:\linebreak {\footnotesize \url{https://github.com/MNR-MarcosOriolPagonabarraga-projects/HMI-EEGMovementClassification.git}}
\end{abstract}
