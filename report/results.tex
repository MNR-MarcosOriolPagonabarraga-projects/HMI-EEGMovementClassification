\subsection{Stratification and accuracy benchmarks}

\label{sec:accuracy}
Models were judged on withheld validation accuracy from the stratified pooled-subject split described in Section~\ref{sec:dataprep}.
CNN checkpoints correspond to maximal validation accuracy during optimisation.
CSP+LDA occupies the pooled training partition for estimation and withheld validation for unbiased accuracy.

  \centering

  \resizebox{\columnwidth}{!}{%
  \begin{tabular}{@{}llc@{}}
    \toprule
    \textbf{Approach series} & \textbf{Representations exploited} & \textbf{Held-out [\%]} \\
    \midrule
    Block~I CSP+LDA~\cite{gramfort2013meg} & motor-band centred CSP~(search over band hypotheses) & \(\sim\)38\,--\,39 \\

    Block~II CNN + PSD~(EEGPSDNet-style) & raw epochs + Welch maps & \(\sim\)61 \\
